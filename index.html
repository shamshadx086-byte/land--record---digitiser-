Land records are the backbone of ownership, tax, and dispute resolution — but most still live on paper.

Registers, cadastral maps, and mutation records sit in handwritten form across village and tehsil offices, in multiple Indian languages, with faded ink and damaged pages. Digitizing them by hand is slow, costly, and error-prone.

Poor source quality. Faded text, damaged pages, and inconsistent formats make standard OCR unreliable.

Multilingual content. Records mix Hindi, regional scripts, and English within the same document.

No verification trail. Manual entry has no confidence signal — errors surface only after they cause disputes.

Siloed systems. Digitized data rarely connects cleanly to LRMS, DILRMP, or GIS platforms already in use.

What the platform does

Every capability maps directly to a requirement in the problem statement — from extraction to governance-ready dashboards.

01

Multilingual OCR

Reads printed and handwritten text across major Indian languages and English in the same document.

02

Field classification

Automatically sorts extracted text into owner, khasra, khata, survey number, village, tehsil, and district fields.

03

Confidence scoring

Every field gets a score, so uncertain extractions are flagged instead of silently accepted.

04

Human-in-the-loop review

Low-confidence fields route to a verification screen where staff can correct values before saving.

05

Progress dashboards

Track documents processed, average accuracy, and pending verification by field and by status.

06

Integration-ready

Structured output is designed to map directly onto LRMS, DILRMP, and GIS data schemas.

How a document moves through the system

Step 01

Upload

Scan or photograph a register page, khatauni, or legacy PDF and upload it directly.

Step 02

Extract

OCR reads the document and structured fields are pulled out automatically.

Step 03

Verify

Fields below the confidence threshold are flagged for a quick human check before saving.

Step 04

Register & report

Verified records land in the register and roll up into the progress dashboard.
